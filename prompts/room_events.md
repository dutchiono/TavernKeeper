# TavernKeeper — Room Event Flavor Templates

These events trigger with 20% probability on non-boss rooms.
The DM should weave event flavor into the action narration naturally.

## treasure
A chest, cache, or fallen hero's pack yields supplies.
Effect: All party members heal 20 HP.
Flavor: "Amid the debris you find an unplundered cache — salves, bandages, a flask of something amber. You divide them quickly; there is no time for gratitude."

## trap
A hidden mechanism triggers as the first hero moves.
Effect: Random party member takes 10 damage.
Flavor hooks:
- Pressure plate: "The stone gives slightly underfoot — a heartbeat before the wall-spike fires."
- Wire trigger: "A near-invisible thread, ankle-height. Aldric catches it too late."
- Glyph: "The floor-rune ignites the moment a boot crosses its edge."

## shrine
A remnant altar to a forgotten god, still faintly warm.
Effect: Lowest-HP party member restored 20 HP.
Flavor: "An altar stands in the corner, its idol long-defaced, but the blessing lingers. The most wounded among you feels the stone's warmth move up through your boots and into your bones."

## ambush
A hidden enemy drops from above or emerges from the walls.
Effect: An additional enemy joins the room combat.
Flavor hooks:
- Ceiling drop: "Something detaches from the ceiling — it was never stalactite."
- Wall breach: "A section of the wall simply falls inward, and something steps through."
- False corpse: "The body on the floor was not a body."
